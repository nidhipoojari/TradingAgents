## Indicator Selection Rationale

To maintain a comprehensive yet non-redundant analytical framework, the selected indicators cover distinct dimensions of market action:
* **`close_50_sma` (Moving Average):** Acts as the primary medium-term trend filter, capturing structural shifts relative to the longer-term 200 SMA (`203.36`) and reacting quicker than macro benchmarks.
* **`rsi` (Momentum):** Measures the speed and change of price movements to isolate near-term exhaustion and approaching oversold/oversold inflection zones without duplicating stochastic noise.
* **`atr` (Volatility):** Quantifies true price range volatility (`6.06`), providing precise inputs for stop-loss placement and position sizing.
* **`vwma` (Volume-Based):** Integrates volume directly into price weighting, confirming whether recent sell-offs or rallies are backed by institutional accumulation or distribution.
